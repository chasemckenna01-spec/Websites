Our Waters

From Māʻalaea to the Maui Channels

We fish the deep offshore drop-offs where ahi, mahi, ono, and marlin run the currents between the islands, and the reef structure closer to shore for bottomfish and inshore action. Where we point the bow depends on the season, the swell, and what’s biting — Captain Chase makes the call every morning.

  • Offshore Grounds — Ahi, mahi mahi, ono, marlin
  • Reef & Inshore — Bottomfish, trevally, snapper
  • Interisland Channels — Seasonal deep trolling
