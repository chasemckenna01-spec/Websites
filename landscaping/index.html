02

How it happens

From bare earth
to first bloom

  1. 1

    Walk the land

    We visit your site, read its light and slopes, and listen to how you want to live outdoors.

  2. 2

    Draw the vision

    Concept sketches become detailed plans — planting palettes, materials, lighting, and water.

  3. 3

    Build & plant

    Our own crews shape the terrain, lay the stone, and set every plant by hand.

  4. 4

    Grow together

    We return through the seasons to prune, renew, and watch the design mature.
